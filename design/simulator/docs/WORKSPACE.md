## Conventions
- **New tools and scripts go in `design/simulator/power/`, measurement output in `power/results/`**, and both are committed.
- **The tree is the design document**, `design/tech_tree.md`, and every tool reads it (`power/defaults.py`). A new version is a commit; to measure an old one, check it out.
- **Parked work goes in `design/notes/deferred_tasks.md`**, and nowhere else.
- **Scratch runs go through `power/scratch_run.sh`.** It copies the document to a private temporary file, applies `scratch_patch.py` to the copy, runs the command against it (`UH_TREE` names the copy) and deletes the copy afterwards. The document is never modified, and parallel runs never share a copy.
- **The finalised design is the default** (`power/defaults.py`), applied wherever a switch isn't set: `LEVERS=abcrwgpnyhukqvVm WROWX=4` (see *Full design and victory time* below), the safety net (`REDISCOVER=0.1 W16=0 ENDUNOCC=1`) and, since v3.122, base Civ V's era timing for development (`DEVENDS=Modern:66231,Atomic:113629,Space:209676 WINTURNS=Modern:47,Atomic:36,Space:56`), late prices `MODERNPRICE=1.25 ATOMICPRICE=1.25 SPACEPRICE=2`, and rivals from `POOLTAG=122`. Set a switch to try a variant; `LEVERS=` (empty) switches every lever off. **A variant that changes prices or eras needs its own rivals** (`make_pool.sh <world> <tag> VAR=value ...`, then `POOLTAG=<tag>`), and tabulating results made under other settings needs their `SPACEPRICE` and `DEVENDS` (empty for v3.121's boundaries).

Labels: `_s1`–`_s3` era prices (round 1); `_d0` the parity setting, `_dw20`, `_dw30`, `_ds8`, `_ds10`, `_dnone` diffusion slowed or switched off at parity prices (round 2, rivals `pb`); `_e1`–`_e7` a dearer Ancient era (round 3; `e6` and `e7` also lengthen the Primitive span, `smooth.py span.Primitive=1.3`, which moves an era's span without its price). Output in `results/smooth_20261015.txt`. The results files and pools aren't kept (about 300 KB a file with discovery turns); `smooth_screen.sh` remakes any of them exactly.
- `endeavour_oracle.py` (15 October): records snapshots of every focus and pairs of ideas studied side by side from benchmark games, then analyses them (usage in its docstring): would an Endeavour get one idea under study sooner than funding its focus, and at less Insight than study, under each of the three costs; and how often the one of two ideas that is behind is discovered first.

- `DRY=1` (15 October): a civilisation with no river or water source. The simulator ignores environmental conditions, so Irrigation is tagged as a world of its own (`*Wet only.*`), and `gen_data.py` leaves it, and every row that cannot be had without it, out of every world.
