Simulated players who decide what to fund only on what the screens show, and the FTL funded-focus rule measured with them
(16 Oct 2026, v3.125). Tamsin: "where a breakthrough isn't revealed, a player has no way to know whether it's occurred or not.
And therefore can't make decisions on what to fund based on that." And: "the expert is allowed to know all *fixed* hidden
information - the shape of the tech tree, prereqs, what is required for each victory, etc. No state, only the fixed
foundations. the novice is not, they only know what's on the screen." And: "I'm more interested in space era ftl Vs non-ftl
techs."

== The audit: where the players used hidden information (harness_ftl_e.py, w7f.py)
- "The most work waiting" (the rivals' choice, the novice's before the unified theory, every player's fallback) counted every
  idea under study, revealed or not.
- A target's focus was funded only once the target's idea had occurred, seen or not (_ftl_active, and the expert's choice).
- The expert's cheapest-target estimate subtracted each idea's progress, which no screen shows (meters stay hidden, §6).
- Target choice and the FTL screen's hints treated an unrevealed idea whose moment had passed as not coming.
- The novice's Endeavour request treated an occurred idea as revealed.
- Not leaks: the Endeavour choices (revealed ideas only), the spread's split between foci (the game's mechanic).

== VISIBLE=1 (default off): every choice on visible information
- Revealed = CORE ideas once discoverable, glimpsed ideas, breakthroughs seen in rivals' hands (theoretical revelation is not
  modelled: the document's Reveals lists are not yet written). Revealed ideas under study count as work.
- The expert keeps the fixed foundations (the tree, prerequisites, prices and multipliers, each method's build and tests, the
  rules, so lock-outs follow from its own discoveries) and visible state (its discoveries, revealed ideas, test results,
  development). An idea it knows of but cannot see counts as possibly under study, so it funds that idea's field; one it can
  see is idle or lost does not; costs are full prices. The novice knows only the screens: the most revealed work under study
  before the unified theory, then the field the FTL screen says progress is possible in; Endeavour requests on revealed ideas.
- Rivals are made under the same setting. The default is unchanged (checked game for game on an expert and a novice cell).
