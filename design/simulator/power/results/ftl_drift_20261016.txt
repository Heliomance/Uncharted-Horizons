Why the FTL methods drifted apart after round two (16 Oct 2026, on v3.125). Tamsin: "the FTL methods previously were in
line with each other with the current shapes. Why have our changes affected some more than others?"

The shapes are unchanged since round two (the build-route rows of v3.112 and v3.125 differ only in RC02 -> RC01, Quantum
Field Theory's prerequisites, and Optimisation Theory's added Programmable Computer). What changed is the cost of a step
taken in sequence. Every Space-era build row is STANDARD: its idea has to occur before it can be studied, and the wait
starts when its prerequisites are met. In a serial chain the waits add up; a row that opens long before it is needed
occurs during the slack and costs nothing. Wormhole, Plenum and Tunnelling end in three Space-era steps one after another,
Warp in two (with an early-opening alternative), Hyperspace in one after an early row, Tachyonic in none.

At round two the simulator's W16 made a Space-era STANDARD idea the player was pursuing occur at once (stronger than the
rule the document wrote: a funded focus keeps its window open, FUNDEDWIN=1, which measures as no rule at all), so a step
cost only its study, 1-12 turns. Three other things also hid the depth: development reached the Space era only around
turn 285, after the races, so every Space-era row waited for the ahead-of-time premium anyway; the players Endeavoured
fresh chain steps; and the rivals diffusion replayed came from an old, fast pool that completed 47-75% of the weaker
players' Hyperspace, Plenum and Tunnelling builds. v3.120 dropped W16 (its cost was shown pooled only: 8 turns for the
expert; per method it was Warp +3.5, Wormhole +11, Hyperspace +7.5, Tachyonic +7, Tunnelling +10, familiar expert). v3.122
doubled the Space-era idea window (WINTURNS: 28 turns for a STANDARD idea instead of 14, so 6.6% a turn instead of 12.7%;
mean wait 9.5 against 4.4 turns if it occurs within the window, about 13 against 7 counting loss and rediscovery), put
development in the Space era from about turn 264, rebuilt the rivals under each version's rules, and capped the expert's
premium (the theory from about 230-244 to 250).
