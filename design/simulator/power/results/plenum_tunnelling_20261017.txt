Plenum finishes 13-15 turns behind its world's field and Tunnelling 7-12.5. The old players (S10O: they saw every idea's
occurrence and progress) had 7 and 4.5-6 on the same design.

Every setting below: all 15 victory conditions, three players, 60 games a cell, 2,700 games; rivals made under the same
switches (make_pool.sh); TOVICTORY=1 SAVE_T=1 PYTHONHASHSEED=0. Spread as in space_endgame_20261016.txt: the mean over worlds
and players of the largest method-to-field deviation, at victory. Each method's figure is its mean deviation from its world's
field (Plenum over the two elemental worlds, Tunnelling in the familiar one). No game in any setting failed to finish.

== 2. Why seeing only the screens slows Plenum and Tunnelling
 (a) The expert's (and the rivals') funding rule. Both use the cheapest-first rule: fund the field holding the cheapest step
     still wanted. With visible information the candidates include every discoverable idea the player cannot see, which may
     not have occurred, so the player funds a field whose wanted idea has not arrived (funding does not make an idea occur)
     while steps it can see waiting elsewhere get only the spread. Plenum suffers most: its Current Sail waits on Rocketry
     and Composites steps that the expert can see but leaves unfunded.
     Plenum's steps, median discovery turn for the expert (Process world):

 (b) Rivals. The rivals are made by the same cheapest-first detective, so under visible information they too are slower to
     these rows, and pass fewer on by diffusion. Share of games in which a row came from rivals:

 (c) The structure. Space-era non-CORE rows that must occur one after another on each method's best route (each waits 10 turns
     on average at 10% a turn, then is studied). After the unified theory: Plenum three (Celestial Current Survey > Current
     Sail x2.5 > Current Rigging), and the Current Sail also needs two more such ideas (Reusable Launch Vehicle, Aerospace
     Industry), which wait on the Rocketry and Composites rows in (a); Wormhole three (Exotic Matter Synthesis or
     Exotic Matter > Exotic Matter Engineering > Transformation Media x4); Warp two. Not waiting on the theory: Tunnelling
     three (Planetary Data Networks > Quantum Computing x2 > Macroscopic Quantum Control). Hyperspace and elemental Tachyonic
     two, atomic Tachyonic one.

== 7. Reading
- The FTL-screen hint is not the cause: following section 4's wording exactly, or a fairer choice between hinted fields,
  changes nothing.
- Most of what seeing only the screens cost Plenum and Tunnelling came from the cheapest-first rule the expert and the
  rivals use. It funds fields for ideas that may not have occurred, leaving visible work waiting, and the rivals then pass
  fewer of these rows on. Funding visible work first (VISPREF) uses no hidden information and gives the expert back the old
  players' pace (Plenum +7.5, not +15), 3 turns quicker overall. That is a question of how the benchmark players are
  modelled, not of the design, and it is Tamsin's to decide.
- What remains is structural: Plenum and Tunnelling each have three Space-era ideas that must occur in sequence, and the
  Current Sail waits on two more. The sync report's two prerequisite changes (P2) take one step out of each, and work with
  either player model: with today's players at parity (303 / 306 / 380, spread 7.4; with Transformation Media too, 6.7),
  and with visible work first to a spread of 4.3, every method within about 4.5 turns of its field.
- Parity with visible work first needs the turns paid back. The Space price, the Atomic price and the Space era's chance a
  turn each buy only 1-2 turns, and all three widen the spread (to 5.3-6.3). The prototype's price buys them cleanly: at
  10x the Space price the package (P2VX10) is at 305 / 306 / 380 with a spread of 4.5.
- The Space era's other content: 13.7-14.6 non-FTL Space-era breakthroughs at the launch under P2VX10, against 19-20 at
  v3.127. Most of the difference comes from funding visible work first, as the old players' 14.6-16.5 shows; the v3.127
  figure was inflated by fields funded for ideas that had not occurred.
