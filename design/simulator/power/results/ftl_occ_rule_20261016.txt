FTL-related ideas occurring as soon as their prerequisites are met while their focus is funded (Tamsin, 16 Oct 2026: "What
happens if specifically ftl-related ideas occur as soon as two things are simultaneously true: all prereqs are met, and the
focus it belongs to is funded? ... include details of how it changes the ratio of ftl-related to non-ftl-related
breakthroughs discovered on the path to victory").

CAVEAT (Tamsin, the same day): the benchmark players choose what to fund partly from hidden information, which ideas have
occurred, though a player cannot know that for an idea that is not revealed. Everything below was measured with the players
as they then were (v3.125's), the baseline included, so the comparisons are like for like; the figures are to be re-measured
with players who decide on what the screens show (VISIBLE, deferred_tasks.md).

The rule as simulated (w7f.py FTLOCC=1; harness_ftl_e.FTL_OCC):
- FTL-related = every breakthrough the FTL screen names in the world: each method's build needs, the breakthroughs that test
  its conditions, the instruments' breakthroughs and the unified theory (atomic 30, of which 25 STANDARD; elemental 26, 22
  STANDARD; the CORE ones already occur at once). It applies from the start of the game, not only once the screen is open.
- Once this turn's funded focus is settled (an Endeavour or the prototype funds none), each of its FTL-related ideas whose
  prerequisites are met occurs at once and takes its share that turn; one whose moment had passed comes back the same way.
- The players use it: choosing a focus, they count the FTL ideas that funding it would make occur as if under study (the
  FTL screen's "progress is possible now in X" invites exactly that).
- Rivals are made under the same rule (make_pool.sh <world> FO FTLOCC=1).
- TOVICTORY=1 plays each game on past the race's finish to its launch turn (victory.py): the prototype takes the funded share
  (80%), the spread goes on, then the SS FTL Drive turns with ordinary research. Races are identical to the stopped games.
